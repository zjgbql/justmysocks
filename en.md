# Just My Socks: BandwagonHost proxy plans and setup

[中文指南](README.md)

Just My Socks (JMS) is a proxy service launched by BandwagonHost and an alternative for people looking for a VPN. Unlike buying a VPS and setting up a proxy yourself, you can use the subscription provided with your plan to configure a compatible client. This is an unofficial guide. Plan links are affiliate links and may earn this project a commission. Check the official checkout for current availability, terms and prices.

## Purchase and setup

1. Open a plan above, select a billing cycle and continue.
2. Apply the promo code and check the plan, initial charge and renewal price.
3. Register and pay, then open **Services → My Services** to find the service's subscription link or connection details.
4. Install a compatible client and import the subscription provided for your service.

The provider has migrated s2–s5 and s801 to VLESS/Reality. Older clients may no longer connect. Use the official [Jamjams app](https://jamjamsapp.com/) or another compatible client, and consult the [official announcements](https://justmysocks3.net/members/index.php?rp=/announcements&language=english). The provider's [software download page](https://justmysocks3.net/members/index.php?rp=/knowledgebase/5/Software-download-links.html&language=english) may require login.
